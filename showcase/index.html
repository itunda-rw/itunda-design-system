QUALITY

Accessibility is part of the component.

IDS treats accessible names, focus, contrast, localization, reduced motion and worst-case content as system contracts.

01

Semantics

Meaningful roles and accessible names are owned by the component and caller where content is product-specific.

02

Resilience

Long Korean, English and Kinyarwanda content, large text and narrow screens are first-class cases.

03

Motion

State changes remain understandable when the platform requests reduced motion.

04

Theming

Client, Business and Developer surfaces share the same foundation without copying the system.
